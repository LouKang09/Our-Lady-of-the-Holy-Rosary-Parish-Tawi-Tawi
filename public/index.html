GRACE FOR THE JOURNEY

Life's sacred
moments.

From new beginnings to lifelong commitments, discover how to take the next step in your sacramental journey.

The following is general guidance. Please confirm the parish's requirements, available dates, and preparation arrangements in person.

Visit the parish →
01BaptismA welcome into the Christian family+

Speak with the parish about arranging a baptism and preparing as parents and godparents.

  • Ask which documents to bring.
  • Confirm the preparation session and godparent requirements.
  • Agree on a date with the parish before making other arrangements.
Plan a parish visit →
02Holy MatrimonyBeginning a lifetime together+

Visit the parish early in your wedding planning to discuss marriage preparation, required documents, and available dates.

  • Ask about the parish's marriage preparation process.
  • Confirm which certificates and other documents are required.
  • A date is confirmed only through the parish; this website does not accept reservations.
Plan a parish visit →
03First Communion & ConfirmationGrowing in faith and discipleship+

Inquire at the parish about catechism, preparation sessions, and the next opportunity to receive First Holy Communion or Confirmation.

Preparation schedules and registration details will be shared when confirmed.

Plan a parish visit →
04Reconciliation & AnointingFinding healing, comfort, and peace+

Ask the parish about confession availability. For a sick or homebound loved one, contact a priest through the parish directly to discuss pastoral care and Anointing of the Sick.

This website does not receive urgent requests.
